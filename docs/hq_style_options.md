The user's attached top-down campus is the reference for this exploration. The alternatives should differ in architectural language, pixel treatment and campus composition, rather than only brightness or roof colours.

## Shared requirements

- Same campus land area across teams; small teams leave facilities unbuilt, top teams occupy all 15 sites.
- Five structural appearances per building, with additions and silhouette changes inside reserved bounds.
- Natural site organisation rather than drawn puzzle plots.
- Racing activity: pit practice, transporters, crew, parts handling and walking staff.
- Clickable buildings, a clear selection state and an inspector with current benefits and next-level preview.
- Independent campus sprites, activity sprites and sharp localisable UI in production.

## Alternatives

| Direction | Architecture and composition | Pixel treatment | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Heritage Works | Converted brick workshops, sawtooth sheds, linked working courts and modern extensions | Restrained earthy palette, chunky practical sprites | Established racing team growing from industrial roots |
| B — Engineering Park | Contemporary roof wings, two landscaped courts and a dedicated logistics spine | Cool coordinated colours, broad graphic geometry | Technical precision and an organised research campus |
| C — Club Racer | Expressive compact garages around pit practice, simple strong roof silhouettes | Larger pixel clusters, fewer colours, flatter shading | More overtly retro game art and immediate racing identity |

These are possible art directions for the whole game. Once one is selected, teams may have different campus layouts and architecture within that coherent style; the three styles need not be mixed between teams. Team campuses retain equal total land area and stable building anchors through upgrades.

## Review criteria

Judge the complete page at normal viewing size: clarity of building selection, racing identity, consistent pixel treatment and whether architecture can support five recognisable levels. The generated concepts are visual studies, not verified 15-building maps or production sprite sheets. Check facility counts and anchors before implementation.

Next, choose one primary direction and name specific elements to retain from the others. Then produce one final campus layout, including all 15 labelled facility anchors, a small-team occupancy version and explicit level 1/3/5 architecture examples at the same scale. Do not begin final asset production until that layout and style are accepted.
