## Prompt

Create a new PIXEL ART game screen concept, landscape 3:2. A complete motorsport team headquarters campus with ALL FIFTEEN buildings visible. Genuine deliberately low-resolution pixel art: large clearly visible square pixels, hard nearest-neighbour edges, limited 32-colour palette, clustered pixel shading, no antialiasing, no gradients, no photographic texture, no realistic rendering, no smooth 3D, no vector art. As if a carefully authored 640x420 pixel-art strategy game scene enlarged exactly 3x. Beautiful confident pixel clusters, readable silhouettes, charming architectural detail, selective dithering only. Elevated isometric game perspective, entire campus boundary and all buildings fit with margins. Rich forest green, petrol blue shadows, warm cream walls, muted terracotta brick, slate blue roofs and amber highlights. Coherent small racing team campus arranged as three neighbourhoods around a modest green courtyard, walking paths and peripheral service road. No huge road grid, no giant monument, no enormous central office. Architecture varied and compact, warm industrial character. Each facility visually distinct and fully visible without occlusion.
