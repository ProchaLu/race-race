# HQ five-level architectural assets

Generated with built-in imagegen, transparent background, one sheet per five facility families. Each sheet contains five levels per family. Runtime uses measured row boundaries, isolates connected building silhouettes, and normalizes them to a shared baseline and source scale. These are distinct architectural sprites, not recoloured/scaled versions of one asset. Final art review remains open.

Files: engineering_levels.png, development_levels.png, operations_levels.png.

## engineering

Production asset sheet for an attractive motorsport campus game. EXACT 5 columns by 5 rows of separate buildings on TRANSPARENT background, equal square cells, strict spacing and 12% transparent gutters. Output square high resolution 2500x2500 if possible. No labels, numbers, UI, ground tiles or text. Camera is orthographic elevated nearly top-down, roofs visible and short front facades, no isometric diamond footprints. Clean refined pixel art, compact deliberate pixel clusters and crisp edges, not chunky 8-bit and not photorealistic. Like an authored modern city-building game rendered with controlled pixel illustration. Rich but restrained architectural detail, coherent navy slate roofs, ivory concrete, blue glass, reddish brick, minimal red motorsport branding. Upper-left light and short coherent shadows. Every sprite fits entirely in its own cell with EMPTY TRANSPARENT GUTTERS. Same camera, same world scale across all cells.
Each COLUMN is a different facility. Each ROW is its architectural LEVEL. Row 1 level 1: modest small dedicated building. Row 2 level 2: actual small extension, changed roof silhouette and equipment. Row 3 level 3: established facility with wing and larger capacity. Row 4 level 4: larger integrated multi-wing engineering complex. Row 5 level 5: substantial sophisticated world-class complex within same cell. IMPORTANT physically design different architecture across all five rows, NOT just bigger scaling or colours. Keep recognisable architectural identity down each column. Higher levels occupy more of the SAME CELL. All sprites must share a coherent campus architectural language. No repeated generic box buildings.
Columns left to right: 1 DESIGN OFFICE stepped L-shaped engineering studio, roof skylights, at high levels joined glazed design wings. 2 WIND TUNNEL test hall with thick silver external U-shaped return duct; at low level small loop and modest hall, high level big enclosed loop plus preparation and control wings. 3 CFD CENTRE server halls, bank of roof cooling fans, high levels additional server wing and cooling plant. 4 FACTORY red brick manufacturing hall with distinctive sawtooth roof bays and roller doors; one bay level1 through five connected bays and loading annex level5. 5 COMPOSITES WORKSHOP clean manufacturing shed with silver horizontal autoclave equipment and glazing, additional controlled-production wings at high levels.
