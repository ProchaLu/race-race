# Facility atlas generation

Tool: built-in imagegen, transparent background.
Output: assets/hq/facilities.png.
The runtime slices a 5-column × 3-row atlas and scales individual sprites with nearest-neighbour sampling. Generated source is a first art pass, not final hand-authored level variants.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. PRODUCTION GAME SPRITE ATLAS on transparent background, exactly FIVE COLUMNS and THREE ROWS of equal-size cells, fifteen separate individual racing headquarters building sprites. No text, no labels, no grid lines, no ground plates. Each sprite fully inside its cell with 12% transparent margin on all sides. All sprites same near-top-down orthographic 2.5D camera, see roof and short south-facing front wall, NO isometric diagonals. Pixel art like a richly crafted 16-bit strategy game: crisp single-pixel outlines, clustered shadows, limited coherent palette, deliberate pixel-level architecture, no antialiasing, no photorealism. Slate blue roofs, ivory walls, reddish brick manufacturing sheds, restrained red livery accents, blue glass, silver engineering equipment. Buildings must feel designed and substantial with roof ridges, glazing, vents, garage doors and purposeful variation, not generic rectangles. Consistent sunlight upper-left and short shadow bottom-right. All cells same pixel scale. No trees or people outside building bounds.
ROW ONE left to right: 1 stepped L-shaped glass design studio with roof skylights; 2 motorsport wind tunnel hall with unmistakable thick rounded silver U-shaped external return duct behind main hall and small glazed control annex; 3 CFD server centre with rooftop bank of four cooling fans; 4 wide red brick sawtooth-roof racing manufacturing factory, four roller loading doors; 5 composites workshop with long silver autoclave cylinders beside hall.
ROW TWO left to right: 6 engine development works with three dyno ventilation towers; 7 pale blue test laboratory with two connected testing annexes; 8 circular racing simulator studio, dark segmented round roof; 9 driver performance gym with glass front and small attached exercise court; 10 U-shaped academy classrooms around small enclosed courtyard.
ROW THREE left to right: 11 small scouting office with distinct pitched roof and skylights; 12 wide race-team garage with four OPEN bays showing dark interiors, red-white race livery stripe, tiny tyre stacks beside doors, no cars in front; 13 mission control operations centre with satellite dishes and short antenna; 14 hospitality pavilion with glazed frontage and roof terrace with a few small parasols; 15 staff canteen with welcoming brick/glass front and small attached patio.
Make exactly fifteen unconnected sprites, neatly centered one in each equal cell, transparent gutters. Canvas aspect ratio FIVE TO THREE, equal square cells. No UI, no campus background, no captions. Pixel art suitable for a real clickable game campus, beautiful cohesive miniature buildings.
